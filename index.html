How to Play Chessdown

🎯 Goal

1. Capture the King: Take the opponent's King.

2. Touchdown: Reach the opponent's Endzone (the last row) with any piece and survive there for one full turn.

🛠️ Draft Phase

You have 39 points to buy your custom army. You must buy exactly 1 King. Place your pieces secretly in your deployment zone and pick your team color.

⏱️ Gameplay

Pieces move exactly like in traditional chess. However, you only have 10 seconds per turn! If the timer runs out, your turn is skipped.

⚠️ Differences with Chess

  • No Checkmate: There is no "check" warning. The King must be physically captured.
  • The Board: It's 8x12, longer than a standard chessboard.
  • Speed: 10 second forced timer!
